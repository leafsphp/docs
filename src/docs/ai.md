Most frameworks weren’t designed for AI.

So you get:

- inconsistent code
- missing context
- broken integrations

Leaf is different.

It gives AI everything it needs to generate clean, working code — every time.

## Why AI struggles with most frameworks

AI doesn’t fail because it’s “bad”
It fails because your app gives it no context

- unclear structure
- inconsistent patterns
- scattered configuration
- missing conventions

So it guesses.

And you debug.

## How Leaf fixes this

Since context is the problem, Leaf gives AI everything it needs:

- a clear structure
- consistent patterns
- centralized configuration
- built-in conventions
- 2-way context sync with AI

## Instant project understanding

Leaf is designed to "talk" to your AI assistant and keep building without context switching. Every fresh install comes with a complete, up-to-date map of your project that AI can understand. So you can just tell your AI what you want to build, and it will install the necessary packages and scaffold the code for you.

Tell your AI:

“Add a Stripe webhook”

And it has everything it needs:

- your routes
- your structure
- your conventions

No broken code. No hallucinated patterns.

The best part? You AI assistant doesn't just read from Leaf's context, it also writes to it after making changes. So you always have an up-to-date map of your project that reflects the latest changes.

## Accurate context, always

Your AI assistant will always sync to Leaf's context, but you can also manually generate a complete, minified map of your project — ready to paste into ChatGPT or any AI assistant from your own changes. To do this, just run:

```bash:no-line-numbers
leaf context
```

No explaining your codebase.
No guessing.
Just accurate context, instantly.

## Predictable structure = better AI output

Leaf enforces consistency:

- clear file locations
- consistent naming
- clean APIs

So AI doesn’t guess — it builds correctly
